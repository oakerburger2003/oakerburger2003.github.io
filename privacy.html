Privacy Policy

Duannee Uploader · last updated 6 November 2026

Who uses this app

Duannee Uploader is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel “เดือนนี้ต้องรอด” to manage that channel. It has no other users.

Data we access

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the app can upload videos to the owner's channel and read the owner's channel details and analytics. It does not access anyone else's account, contacts, email or files.

How the data is used and stored

Videos and their titles and descriptions are sent to YouTube only to publish them on the owner's channel. Channel statistics are read only to help the owner decide what to make next. The sign-in token is stored privately in the owner's own workspace and is never shared or sold. No data is shared with third parties other than Google/YouTube.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
